Contributing to the Idea Bank

The Idea Bank exists to make ideas available for people to explore and develop.

You do not need permission from the original creator to work with an idea, provided you follow the applicable license terms.

You may

- Use an idea
- Modify it
- Develop it further
- Combine it with other ideas
- Build products or services from it
- Commercialize it
- Sell what you build
- Adapt it for another purpose
- Build something substantially different from the original concept

Attribution

The ideas and their documentation are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license.

Attribution is required under the license.

Please attribute the original idea as:

Original idea by Rakshitha Prakash at The Ecstatic Alchemists Universe.

IG and Threads: @theecstaticalchemistsuniverse

Where practical, link back to the original Idea Bank entry.

If You Build Something

You are encouraged, but not required beyond the license terms, to document what you created.

Useful information may include:

- Which idea you used
- What you changed
- What you built
- What worked
- What did not work
- What you learned
- Where the resulting project can be found

The purpose is to create a trail showing how an idea developed after being released.

Adding Information

If you notice an error in an Idea Bank entry, you may open an issue or submit a proposed change.

Please do not rewrite the original creator's history or intentions. If you have developed the idea further, document your version separately or clearly identify what you changed.
