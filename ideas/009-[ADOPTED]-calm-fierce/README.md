## Origin

Calm + Fierce developed as a positioning and conceptual direction within Wild Womaniya Studio.

## The Problem

The idea responds to the assumption that calmness must necessarily mean softness, passivity, gentleness, or stillness.

## The Idea

Combine **calm** and **fierce** as complementary qualities.

Calm does not have to mean passive, and fierce does not have to mean chaotic.

The concept allows a person or product to hold both qualities at the same time.

## Why This Idea Exists

The direction emerged from the development of body-first work and the user's preference for vigorous, energetic approaches rather than exclusively gentle calming practices.

**Calm + Fierce**

with both qualities treated as compatible rather than mutually exclusive.

The concept is intentionally broad and was not defined as a clinical framework.
