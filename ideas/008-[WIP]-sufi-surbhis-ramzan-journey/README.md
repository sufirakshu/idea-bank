## Origin

Sufi Surbhi's Ramzan Journey was developed as a storytelling concept within the Wild Womaniya ecosystem.

## The Problem

The idea explores how a personal or fictional journey can be used to communicate experiences, reflections, and practices through an ongoing story.

## The Idea

Create a story centered around Sufi Surbhi's journey through Ramzan.

The journey can serve as a narrative framework through which experiences, reflections, practices, and related material can be presented.

## Why This Idea Exists

The concept provides a narrative vehicle for exploring Ramzan through a character's journey rather than presenting the material only as standalone instructions or informational content.
