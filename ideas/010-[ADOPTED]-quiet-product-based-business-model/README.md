# [ADOPTED] IDEA 010 — Quiet Product-Based Business Model

## Status

ADOPTED

## Origin

This idea developed from discussions about creating a business around products rather than ongoing live service delivery.

## The Problem

Traditional service businesses can require repeated calls, meetings, live sessions, appointments, and continuous direct interaction with clients.

The idea explores a business structure where products can be created once and distributed without requiring the creator to be continuously present for delivery.

## The Idea

Build a quiet, product-based business in which the primary work consists of creating, documenting, publishing, and selling products rather than providing ongoing live services.

The business can include digital products, written material, creative resources, and other products that can be delivered without scheduled live interaction.

## Why This Idea Exists

The concept developed from a preference for work that can be created independently and delivered without calls, webinars, summits, or scheduled live sessions.

## Who Is It For?

### Primary

Creators who want to build businesses around products rather than primarily around live services.

### Secondary

Customers who prefer purchasing and using products independently.

## Context

The idea developed within the Wild Womaniya Studio ecosystem and its product, publishing, and creative-resource work.

## What Already Exists

The product-based business model was established as a direction for Wild Womaniya Studio.

Various digital products, written resources, and creative product concepts were explored within this model.

## What Makes This Idea Distinct

The distinguishing characteristic is the emphasis on products that can be created and delivered without requiring continuous live participation from the creator.

## What Has Already Been Done

- The product-based business direction was adopted.
- Digital products and written resources were explored.
- Publishing structures were developed.
- The model was connected to independent, asynchronous work.

## What Has NOT Been Done

No single complete business system implementing every possible aspect of this model has been documented here.

## Minimum Viable Version

A minimum version consists of:

1. One product.
2. A way to present the product.
3. A way for customers to purchase it.
4. A delivery mechanism that does not require a live session.

## How Someone Could Build It

Someone could:

1. Identify a product that can be created independently.
2. Create and package the product.
3. Publish it through an appropriate platform.
4. Provide a purchase and delivery mechanism.
5. Use written or asynchronous communication where customer support is needed.

The exact tools and platforms were not fixed.

## Resources / Research

The idea developed from the Wild Womaniya business-model discussions.

Specific platforms were explored at different points, but no single platform is required by this idea.

## Open Questions

- Which products should be included?
- What distribution platform should be used?
- What level of customer support is appropriate?
- How much automation is desirable?
- Which products should remain independent?

## Risks / Limitations

A product-based model still requires product creation, distribution, discovery, customer support, and other business activities.

It does not eliminate all ongoing work.

## Development History

The concept developed as an alternative to business models dependent on live delivery.

It became part of the broader Wild Womaniya approach to digital products, publishing, and independent creative work.

## Current State

ADOPTED.

The quiet product-based business model was adopted as a business direction.
