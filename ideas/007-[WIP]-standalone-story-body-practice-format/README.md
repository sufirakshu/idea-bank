## Origin

## The Problem

Instruction-only tools can feel disconnected from the person's lived experience.

The idea explores combining a story with a practical body-based exercise so that the reader can encounter an idea through both narrative and physical experience.

## The Idea

Create standalone pieces that combine:

1. A story or narrative.
2. A body-based practice or exercise connected to the story.

Each piece can function independently rather than requiring the reader to consume a larger sequence.

## Why This Idea Exists

The format creates a bridge between written storytelling and practical body-based experience.

It also allows individual stories and practices to become standalone pieces of content or products.

## Who Is It For?

### Primary

The standalone story-plus-practice format was identified as a distinct content/product format.

## What Makes This Idea Distinct

The story and body practice are intentionally connected and presented together as a standalone unit.

## What Has Already Been Done

- The format was conceptualized.
- It was connected to Wild Womaniya's storytelling and body-based work.
- Individual story concepts were explored within the broader ecosystem.

## What Has NOT Been Done

A complete standardized template for every story-plus-practice piece has not been documented.

## Minimum Viable Version

One standalone piece containing:

- A complete short story.
- One related body practice.
- Simple instructions for the practice.

## How Someone Could Build It

A creator could:

1. Write a short story around a particular experience or theme.
2. Identify a body-based practice that relates to the story.
3. Present the story and practice together.
4. Make the resulting piece usable independently.

The final publishing format was not fixed.
