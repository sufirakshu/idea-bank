Rakshitha Prakash / The Ecstatic Alchemists Universe / IG and Threads @theecstaticalchemistsuniverse

## Origin

Wild Womaniya Studio originated as an idea for a product and creative studio.

The idea developed through discussions about creating digital products, publishing, creative tools, and other forms of work under the Wild Womaniya name.

## The Problem

The idea was developed around creating an independent studio through which different kinds of creative and digital work could be developed and presented without requiring a conventional agency or service-business model.

## The Idea

Wild Womaniya Studio is a product-oriented creative studio for developing and publishing digital products, creative resources, written work, tools, and other projects.

The studio was conceived as a container for multiple kinds of creative work rather than as a single product or service.

## Why This Idea Exists

The studio provided a structure through which different ideas, products, experiments, and creative projects could exist under one umbrella.

## Who Is It For?

### Primary

People who may use, purchase, read, or interact with the products and resources created through the studio.

### Secondary

People interested in collaborating with, developing, or building upon projects created through the studio.

## Context

Wild Womaniya Studio evolved through multiple discussions and experiments around digital products, publishing, creative resources, and ways of working independently.

The studio was also connected with a broader idea of creating work across multiple formats and media.

## What Already Exists

The studio was established as a named project/brand.

Various ideas, products, publishing structures, and creative formats were developed under or in connection with it.

## What Makes This Idea Distinct

The studio was conceived as a flexible container for multiple forms of creative and product-based work rather than being limited to one product, service, medium, or format.

## What Has Already Been Done

- Wild Womaniya Studio was established as a named project.
- Digital product ideas were developed under the studio.
- Publishing concepts and structures were developed.
- Creative resources and product concepts were explored.
- Multiple media and formats were considered as part of the studio ecosystem.

## What Has NOT Been Done

Not every concept developed under Wild Womaniya Studio was built, published, or commercially released.

The full scope of the studio remained evolving.

## Minimum Viable Version

A minimum version of the idea consists of:

1. A clearly defined studio identity.
2. A place to present its work.
3. Individual products, resources, or projects that can exist independently.
4. A method for publishing or distributing those individual works.

## How Someone Could Build It

Someone developing this idea could establish a creative/product studio with:

- A studio identity.
- A public home for its work.
- Individual products or projects.
- A publishing/distribution system.
- Documentation for each project.
- Different media or formats as appropriate to each project.

The exact implementation was not fixed.

## Resources / Research

Relevant resources, tools, and platforms were discussed during the development of Wild Womaniya Studio, including tools for creating, publishing, hosting, and distributing digital products.

Specific implementation choices changed over time.

## Open Questions

- What should ultimately belong under the Wild Womaniya Studio umbrella?
- Which projects should remain independent?
- Which formats and media should be developed further?
- What should the long-term structure of the studio be?

## Risks / Limitations

- A broad studio structure can contain many different projects and therefore may remain difficult to define precisely.
- Not every project developed under the studio is necessarily active.
- The studio's structure and scope changed over time.

## Development History

Wild Womaniya Studio began as a broader creative and product-oriented studio concept.

Over time, individual products, publishing ideas, creative resources, and other projects were developed within or alongside the studio.

The studio also evolved into a broader multi-medium ecosystem, including written work, digital products, creative resources, and other forms of media.

Some concepts were built, some remained works in progress, and some were paused or changed.

## Current State

ADOPTED.

Wild Womaniya Studio was adopted as a working creative/product studio concept, although individual projects within it have had different development states.
